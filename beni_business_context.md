# Business Context: White Floral Beni Landing Page

## Overview

This business sells a single product: a handcrafted white floral hair accessory called a "Beni".

A Beni is worn around a hair bun (খোঁপা) and is commonly used by women to enhance traditional hairstyles for special occasions.

The primary sales channel is Facebook Ads.

The goal is to drive traffic from Facebook directly to a single-product landing page and convert visitors into buyers through an order form or WhatsApp order flow.

---

# Product

## Product Name

Handmade White Floral Beni

## Category

Women's Hair Accessory

## Style

Traditional, Elegant, Feminine

## Product Appearance

- Circular floral bun accessory
- White flower design
- Looks similar to fresh white flowers
- Worn around a hair bun
- Lightweight
- Reusable
- Handmade

---

# Target Customer

## Primary Audience

Women in Bangladesh who:

- Wear sarees
- Attend weddings
- Attend family events
- Attend cultural programs
- Participate in dance performances
- Like traditional fashion
- Enjoy dressing up for special occasions
- Care about photos and appearance

## Emotional Drivers

Customers are NOT buying a hair accessory.

Customers are buying:

- Beauty
- Confidence
- Elegance
- Attention
- Compliments
- Better photos
- A complete traditional look

---

# Brand Positioning

The product should NOT be positioned as:

- Artificial flower accessory
- Cheap hair accessory
- Generic fashion item

Instead position it as:

"An elegant finishing touch that completes a beautiful hairstyle."

Core message:

"A small detail that transforms the entire look."

---
